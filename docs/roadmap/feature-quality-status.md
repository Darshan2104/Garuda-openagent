## Remaining acceptance work

The four follow-ups are reviewable increments. Keep the parent issues open until
their complete acceptance criteria and exact merged revisions are verified.

- **Spend:** dollar limits remain stops based on reported spend, rather than
  prepaid exact maxima. Inspection/usage views now show limits and scope,
  settled/reserved tokens, unknown-cost counts and resume inheritance through
  `sessions show` and the dashboard. Known dollars are labelled as a lower bound
  when cost is unknown; missing or unreadable snapshots never imply zero. Crash/resume reservation provenance is covered by
  admission/settlement checkpoints and interrupted-reservation estimate charges
  with unknown cost. Cancelled and unknown-outcome failed calls without usage
  now charge their reservation with unknown cost; definite rejections release
  it without charge. Calls with reported usage or a successful completion
  sum tokens per attempt; each non-rejected failed attempt without usage
  adds the largest positive reported prompt size, or the reservation estimate
  if unavailable, with unknown cost. Estimated-token and unknown-cost calls
  count once per call; whole-call failure estimates receive no extras. This
  lower bound excludes unreported output generated by a failed stream. A
  dollar-only cap stops subsequent calls after an unreported transient retry;
  use `--max-run-tokens` to bound continued work. External ACP model calls and
  pre-send refusal classification remain outside native spend enforcement.
- **Investigation:** preserve read-only collection briefs where sufficient.
  Preview is inspection, not a reservation: execution must independently
  revalidate the source and current workspace. Context rewind restores neither
  files nor processes. The completion judge enforces exclusion of fork-inherited
  history: historical checks never verify the new candidate. Compaction summaries
  of inherited history stay marked and excluded; current summaries and running
  notes use only current inputs. Repeated forks retain earlier source IDs and
  compaction markers while naming the immediate source. Agent-initiated
  `buffer_grep`/`buffer_slice` reads
  of archived inherited text remain fresh observations of historical text; the
  archive pointer warns about inherited counts and source sessions.
- **Workers:** bounded programmatic editing fan-out is supported with exclusive
  serialized integration; siblings remain stale after an integrated change.
  Model-driven fan-out and non-local worker backends remain open, and approval
  prompts are not serialized. Capacity contention no longer quarantines a parent
  when store reads and workspace absence prove ownership was never acquired. Closing known
  environment services does not prove all descendant cleanup. Keep uncertain
  repositories, leases and capacity quarantined. Live Docker integration checks
  require separate evidence.
- **Streaming:** native terminal runs and interactive chat show parent previews.
  Child deltas are SDK-only opt-in (`subagent_deltas=True`), labelled by source,
  agent and depth; terminal and dashboard ignore them. Auxiliary (intentionally),
  consult and collection calls remain outside this forwarding path. Interrupted
  streams keep reported partial usage as a lower bound. No latency or quality
  improvement is inferred from tool overlap or fixture speed.

## Validation owners

Use `tests/test_collection_integration.py` and `tests/test_spend_gate.py` for root
admission/accounting; `tests/test_sessions_resume.py` and
`tests/test_investigation_scope.py` for fork and report boundaries;

`tests/test_web_live_runs.py` with `tests/test_loop_streaming.py` for dashboard
delivery and loop semantics. The existing
[live Chrome chat check](../development/browser-checks.md) exercises rendered
previews, completed responses, approvals and grounding without a paid provider.

Record focused checks, full-suite results, dependency/install conditions and
unavailable integrations in each PR. The campaign tracker is the rollup, not a
fifth runtime feature. Close it only after the remaining implementation issues
have their own acceptance evidence.
